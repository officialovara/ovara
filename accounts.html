Your Accounts

Four currencies. One OVARA account.

Manage your supported currency balances from one secure place. Select an account to view its balance, account details and available actions.

🔐 Currency balances shown here are currently part of the OVARA prototype. Real account balances, deposits, withdrawals, transfers and receiving details will be connected through secure financial providers and the OVARA backend.
OVARA supports four currencies only: NGN, USD, GBP and EUR.
